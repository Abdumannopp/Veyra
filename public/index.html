IntegrationsAI agents, SMS / WhatsApp and GPS
Your company's Twilio account Messages are sent from your own Twilio account and number. The token is checked with Twilio, stored encrypted and never shown again.

Allowed senders Only emails from these addresses or domains are read. Leave empty to accept everyone who knows your inbox address.

Order mailbox
Or connect with a mail server and an app password (IMAP): VEYRA reads new emails of this mailbox itself, as if they were sent to your inbox address. Mail already in the mailbox is not imported, and nothing in it is changed or marked as read. Use an app password (Gmail, Outlook) and leave the password empty to keep the saved one.

Border waiting times (for ROUTE) The usual wait at a border, in hours. One border per line: two country codes and hours, for example UZ; KZ; 6. Place names must end with the country code ("Tashkent, UZ"). Used when the lane has no history yet.

Market prices (for PULSE) Prices from your broker or a freight exchange. One lane per line: Origin; Destination; Equipment (optional); Price. A line for the same lane replaces the old price.
